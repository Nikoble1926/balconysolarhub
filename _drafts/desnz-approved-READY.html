What DESNZ announced

On [[DATE]], the Department for Energy Security and Net Zero (DESNZ) [[WHAT_EXACTLY]]. It follows the public consultation that opened on 16 June 2026 and closed on 30 June 2026, and it is the first concrete step turning the proposal into a workable legal route for plug-in (balcony) solar in the UK.

The legal mechanism is the one consulted on: an amendment to the Plugs and Sockets (Safety) Regulations 1994 (PSSR), supported by the DESNZ Interim Product Specification, with an enduring (BSI) standard to follow. Read the primary document here: GOV.UK — DESNZ plug-in solar publication ([[DATE]]).

The device limits

The headline limit is [[CAP_W]]. Within the framework consulted on, a compliant plug-in solar device is expected to meet:

  • Single phase, ≤253V AC / 50Hz.
  • Maximum apparent power ≤800VA; maximum current ≤3.5A.
  • PV DC ≤2000W.
  • One device per household; no batteries.
  • BS 1363 plug with a 5A fuse and partially insulated pins.
