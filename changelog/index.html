Changelog — UK plug-in solar transition

BS 7671 Amendment 4   Tracker last updated: 16 August 2026

This page tracks the UK plug-in / balcony solar regulatory transition under BS 7671 Amendment 4 and the updates we make to this site. Dates that have happened are shown plainly; future milestones are marked expected because they are anticipated, not confirmed. Newest first.

Anticipated (not yet confirmed)

DateMilestoneWhat it means
~ October 2026 expectedEnd of the transitional periodBy this point the legal and product-standard picture for DIY plug-in kits should be coherent end-to-end. Anticipated, not confirmed.
16 July 2026Regulations made — SI 2026 No. 848The Plugs and Sockets etc. (Safety) Regulations 1994 and Electricity Safety, Quality and Continuity Regulations 2002 (Amendment) Regulations 2026 were made, and laid before Parliament on 17 July 2026. They come into force on 27 August 2026.

Happened

DateMilestoneWhat it means
30 June 2026 closedDESNZ plug-in solar consultation closed (23:59)The public consultation on legalising plug-in solar has closed. DESNZ is analysing responses; the regulations were made on 16 July 2026 and come into force on 27 August 2026. DIY plug-in is not legal before that date.
16 June 2026 openedDESNZ opened the consultation to legalise plug-in solarGovernment formally proposed legalisation via a PSSR amendment + DESNZ Interim Product Specification v1.0 (written statement HCWS118). Consultation stage — not yet legal. Details.
16 June 2026 siteAdded UK guides: renters, planning permission, and G98 (UK Power Networks)Expanded the practical guidance set for renters and the DNO notification route.
15 June 2026 siteSite launched — honest BS 7671 Amendment 4 guideFirst publication of BalconySolarHub, framing what is actually legal today during the transition.
15 April 2026 in forceBS 7671 Amendment 4 came into forceMicrogeneration up to 800W is now classed as a portable appliance, provided the safety requirements are met (800W AC cap, single-phase, type-approved anti-islanding inverter, RCD protection).
16 March 2026BS 7671 Amendment 4 ratified by the IETIndustry signal that the rule change was final. No live effect until it came into force on 15 April 2026.

Site and dataset

Changes to what this site publishes, kept separate from the legal milestones above because they are not the same kind of event.

DateChangeWhat it means
2 September 2026Dataset v1.4 — the DIY notification note closesThe ENA published a registration portal at myplugin.solar. Read 2 September 2026, its FAQ states that registering a device there sends the notification to the DNO. applies_to_diy_householder stays null because EREC G98 is unchanged, but its note now records the route, with the legal-requirement claim attributed to the portal rather than asserted by us. The Zenodo deposit remains at v1.3.
16 August 2026New guide: G98 protection settings at 253 VPublishes the trip points and the 90 minute operating range test from a type test certificate issued against Engineering Recommendation G98, and sets them against the published output range of an EU-edition APsystems EZ1-M. Read it. We have not read G98 itself; the page says so.
2 August 2026Zenodo DOI, and the G98 page rewrittenThe DNO dataset is deposited at Zenodo and has a concept DOI, so /developers/ and /g98-notification/ now cite a permanent identifier. /g98-notification/ was rewritten from the dataset rather than edited, because what was there described the notification process as free and self-serve.
1 August 2026DNO dataset published, then corrected twice/data/uk-plug-in-solar.json publishes SI 2026 No. 848 verbatim alongside the G98 requirements of all six DNOs across fourteen licence areas, with null where no primary source states a figure, plus a per-operator split with an MPAN 10–23 lookup. v1.1 corrected a material error: the 28-day deadline is national, set by EREC G98 §1.1 under ESQCR 22(2)(c), not each operator’s own policy. v1.2 and v1.3 added evidence from the operators’ own pages, and the visible version chip was corrected from 1.0 to 1.3. /data/ and /data/dno/ previously returned the homepage with HTTP 200 and now have real index pages.
