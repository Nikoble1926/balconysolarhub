Put the register table on your own page

Four devices were listed under the plug-in solar filter of the ENA Type Test Register on 5 August 2026. Three were listed on 14 August. One reference disappeared and its page stopped loading, and two were republished under new references. That is the point of this table: it is a dated reading of a register that moves, and it carries the date and time it was taken.

Every reference links to its own page on the register, so a reader can check what it says today rather than trusting our copy of it.

The code

<iframe src="https://balconysolarhub.co.uk/embed/ena-register/"
        width="100%" height="700"
        style="border:1px solid #ddd;border-radius:8px"
        title="ENA Type Test Register: plug-in solar devices"
        loading="lazy"></iframe>

Nothing else is needed. No script, no key, no account. The table and the reading date sit in a bar that stays at the top of the widget, so they are visible without scrolling whatever the height of your column.

Optional: automatic height

<iframe id="bsh-ena" src="https://balconysolarhub.co.uk/embed/ena-register/"
        width="100%" height="700" style="border:1px solid #ddd;border-radius:8px"
        title="ENA Type Test Register: plug-in solar devices" loading="lazy"></iframe>
<script>addEventListener('message',function(e){
 if(e.origin!=='https://balconysolarhub.co.uk')return;
 if(e.data&&e.data.bshHeight)document.getElementById('bsh-ena').style.height=e.data.bshHeight+'px';});</script>

Keep the origin check. The line comparing e.origin against our domain is not optional: without it your page would act on messages from any frame, and that is a hole we would have opened in your site.

What we do not promise

The table is what the register showed at the date and time printed inside the widget. It is not a live feed and it is not our assessment: “Non-compliant” is the register’s own status, quoted with its own explanation. The register changes, sometimes within days, which is why the reading date is part of the widget and not a footnote.

We have not matched any of these model numbers to a retail product, and you should not assume they correspond to anything sold under a brand name.
