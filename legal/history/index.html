What changed on the ENA type test register, and when

What this is: the ENA type test register decides which plug-in solar devices are accepted onto UK networks, and it moves — but neither it nor anyone else publishes what changed between one day and the next. We read it on set dates through its own search API and record every reading here, append-only. Latest reading: 2 September 2026 at 11:06. The current snapshot lives on our UK legal page.

Readings before 19 August 2026 are recorded with their counts and a note; we did not capture full device references at the time, and we do not reconstruct them here. From 19 August 2026 onward each reading lists every device reference in full.
